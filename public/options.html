Everything saves automatically and stays on this device. No accounts, analytics, or remote rule updates.

Quick filters

Common kinds of listings people disagree about. Turn one off if you actually want to see those listings — for example, keep liquidation stores or IKEA resales.

Rule groups

Whole detection areas. Turning a group off disables every rule inside it.

All built-in rules

Full transparency: every detector, its exact pattern, weight, and confidence. Toggle off any rule that is too aggressive for how you shop.

Rule tester

Paste any listing text to see exactly what SlopBlock would do with your current settings — before you tweak anything.

My terms

Your own local rules, one word or phrase per line. Terms match whole words or phrases, never fragments inside other words.

Backup & reset

Export/import all settings as JSON — useful for sharing rule presets between machines without any account.
